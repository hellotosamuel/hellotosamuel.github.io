The rating scale

1 · Does not demonstrate at all
6 · Consistently and strongly demonstrates

Each row is one review, with five required integer ratings. For individual reports, include the name of the person being reviewed. Repeated names are expected in round-robin reviews.

Paste your responses

Copy the five rating columns, or include the rest of your sheet and choose which columns to use.

Check your columns

Select the person being reviewed, not the reviewer. Repeated names are grouped into one person, with each quality averaged across all reviews received. Use the same spelling for a person throughout the sheet.

Individual reports
